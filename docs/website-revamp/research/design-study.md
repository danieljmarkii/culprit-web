# Culprit Marketing Site Revamp — Design Research Brief

_Research by the Sr. Product Designer lens: consensus scan (Godly, Minimal Gallery, Landingfolio, DesignRush, Framer gallery/awards, Awwwards, pet-DTC roundups), then live capture of 17 reference sites + the current getculprit.app (desktop 1440 full-page, mobile 390×844 fold, plus a computed-styles probe per site: fonts, sizes, grounds, page heights, CTAs, section order). Calm.com blocked automation; everything else captured. Feeding the 2026-07-09 roundtable._

## 1. The "before": getculprit.app today

Night-indigo hero (centered mark + wordmark, Newsreader H1 at **48px**, Geist body), cream problem band, white 3-step band, indigo pricing band. Total height **2,368px** — the shortest page studied except Retro. The audit makes four things unmissable:

- **Zero images, zero video, zero product UI.** Every reference site leads with product evidence; Culprit has none.
- **No funnel.** Zero store links; the hero pill is not a link; only interactive elements are legal links + a mailto.
- **No social proof of any kind.**
- **Type scale an era below benchmark.** Reference heroes run 52–148px; 48px reads as an article headline, not a display statement.

What's already right: the copy voice, the night/cream banding rhythm, small-caps kickers, teal used only functionally. The bones are on-brand; the site is under-produced and un-funneled.

## 2. Key teardowns (compressed)

- **Flighty** (single-app benchmark, ADA winner): announcement bar → persistent "Get the app" pill → 65px H1 → **Apple laurels directly under the sub** → photographed hand holding phone with real UI, orbited by floating notification cards; press logo wall; mono for data moments; 14,462px of Framer maximalism. *Steal: laurel/press placement, notification-card orbit (sedated), persistent nav CTA. Avoid: the volume.*
- **Copilot Money**: near-black navy hero, 148px display, five laurels; **the class-leading mobile fold** (logo → full H1 → sub → one CTA → laurels in screen one); palette inversion as chapter break; bento UI fragments. *Steal: mobile fold recipe, "honest pricing" framing. Avoid: gradient type, glossy 3D.*
- **Retro**: the whole site is one 900px viewport — logo, one serif line, one photo, store badges. *Lesson: badge as first-class design element; brutal brevity needs pre-existing reputation.*
- **Halide**: near-black, floating devices, **App Store badge restyled in the brand accent**; body is proof-by-artifact (photo essay, before/afters). *Steal: the report/Signal shown as beautiful artifacts at scale; accent-colored store CTA.*
- **Things 3**: tilted oversized UI card as hero art; device family; real badges with visible prices; masonry love-wall of social posts. *Steal: tilted oversized UI; transparent platform cards.*
- **Linear**: rgb(8,9,10), 64px tuned Inter, **UI dissolving into the ground via glow/vignette (no bezel)**; two-tone statement sentences; spec-style figure numbering ("1.0 Intake →", "fig 0.2"); Berkeley Mono annotations; monochrome logo row. *Steal: bezel-less UI on night; figure numbering (extends our 01/02/03); two-tone sentences → Newsreader roman + muted cream.*
- **Raycast**: black + ONE red accent; grain-textured painterly hero streaks; statement couplets ("It's not about saving time. / It's about feeling like you're never wasting it."); mono microcopy. *Steal: couplet cadence; one-accent discipline; textured ambient hero art → **the Whorl finally gets its marketing job**.*
- **Vercel/Stripe**: mono small-caps as a labeling layer; one ownable ambient hero graphic owned for a decade.
- **Oura** (the register benchmark): full-bleed macro photo hero; **Editorial New serif at 110px/300** with *italic serif phrases inside roman headlines*; warm cream fields; "IN THE NEWS" dark press-card band; 5,563px; scroll-driven storytelling (redesign case study: +42% sales). *Steal: nearly everything tonally — this is the closest published analogue to Moon & Signal.*
- **Headspace**: checkmark microproof row under H1; **intent chips** that self-segment visitors; tabbed features. *Steal: intent chips → "On an elimination trial / Watching a symptom / Getting ready for the vet"; microproof row. Avoid: primary-color cheer.*
- **Fi** (pet-tech): cinematic B/W dog videography, one yellow accent carrying every action, mono telemetry labels — and the full DTC pressure kit (urgency bar, discount modal). *Steal: one-accent proof, cinematic-not-cute pet photography. Avoid: every urgency mechanic.*
- **The Farmer's Dog**: warm lifestyle hero; **four-cell trust band mixing kinds of proof, each with one line of context** (Cornell + TODAY + Google reviews + CBS); "vet developed" framing; evidence-forward but never diagnostic. *Steal: contextualized trust band — the strong form of proof for health-adjacent brands.*
- **Sundays for Dogs**: **the typographic cousin** — Garamond Light 52px display + grotesk body + Sackers small-caps kickers + italic serif press quotes on cream with one accent (swap cream→moonlight, green→indigo, yellow→teal and it's our palette logic at high polish); annotated-artifact pattern (macro shot with small-caps callouts); guarantee chips at CTA. *Steal: small-caps kicker system; annotated artifact; guarantee chips → honesty chips. Avoid: discount bar.*
- **Amie / (Not Boring)**: even design darlings converge on proof-sequencing; mono catalog-numbering rhymes with clinical numbering. The maximalist-playful pole locates where Culprit is not.

## 3. Cross-cutting patterns (what the best of 2025–26 share)

1. **Real product UI is non-negotiable** — in-hand photos, device families, bezel-less UI fading into the ground, or UI fragments in bento cards. Nobody ships text-only.
2. **Proof within the first scroll, contextualized** — laurels under the sub; mixed trust bands with one-line captions. Uncaptioned logo walls are the weak form.
3. **Dark sites are black; cream sites are warm — almost nobody owns both.** Culprit's night-indigo + moonlight two-act page is genuinely differentiated; Copilot's palette-inversion-as-chapter-break is the pattern to copy.
4. **One accent, ruthlessly functional** — the era's discipline matches "the Signal dot is never decorative" exactly.
5. **Display type is enormous and confident** — 52–148px heroes, often light-weight serifs; editorial serif + grotesk body + **monospace annotation layer** is the reigning three-part stack.
6. **Signature copy devices** — two-tone sentences, statement couplets, italic serif flourishes, numbered spec sections. Quiet-confidence tools.
7. **Motion is scroll-revealed and product-centered**, not decorative parallax.
8. **Mobile fold formula** — complete pitch in screen one + persistent nav CTA pill.
9. **Page lengths cluster ~5,500–15,000px**; grammar: hero → proof → 3–5 chapters → deeper trust → pricing/honesty → final CTA + badge.
10. **Health-adjacent brands add pragmatic-trust furniture** — studies, "vet-developed" language, guarantee chips; trust through practical honesty, not hype.

## 4. What transfers to Culprit / what fights the brand

**Adopt:** Oura's register wholesale (light 90–110px Newsreader, italic flourishes, cream warmth, one calm CTA); a Linear/Raycast night hero *warmed* (the Whorl as huge faint grain-textured crescent-fingerprint — its designated marketing job; bezel-less app UI dissolving into night); Flighty's chip orbit *sedated* (static matte log chips + exactly one teal Signal chip — the brand promise drawn as a diagram); Copilot's mobile fold + Flighty's persistent nav pill; Halide's accent-badge trick at launch; Farmer's-Dog-style contextualized trust band sized to what's true; the clinical-report motif as a design system (figure numbering + mono/small-caps annotation layer; the vet report shown at Halide scale is Culprit's strongest single image); Sundays' kickers and honesty chips; length target ~5,000–6,500px.

**Reject:** urgency bars, discounts, capture modals, inflated counts; gradient-fill/glossy-3D display type; pitch-black grounds and all-caps heroes (our night is indigo, sentence-case); cheerful primaries/mascots; any second accent; Retro-style silence (pre-reputation, we must still explain).
